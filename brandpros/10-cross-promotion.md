## Ground rules
1. **Collab, don't copy.** Never upload the same video file to two brands' channels. YouTube and Meta both penalize reused content. Each brand makes **its own version from its own angle** and they link to each other.
2. **It has to fit naturally.** Max 1–2 cross-promos per week per brand, so audiences don't feel "sold to".
3. **Each brand keeps its own voice.** historypros = the era and its impact; financeprostv = what it means for your wallet; BrandPros = the business story and the decision.
4. **Use the platform-native collab tools**, which give both accounts credit and reach.

| Mechanic | Where | How to use it |
