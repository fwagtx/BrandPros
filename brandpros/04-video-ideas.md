# 04 — 100 Video Ideas

⭐ = **Top 20** (strongest hook plus broad appeal plus a documented story plus a long-form path). Start here.
📜 = full Short script already written in `05-scripts/`. 🎬 = long-form outline in `06-longform-outlines/`.
**[VERIFY]** = the number or claim must be confirmed against a primary source before publishing (see the fact-check step in `08-production-workflow.md`).
👤 = involves a living person or current company, so the legal wording rules in `01-brand-identity.md` apply.

Each idea includes a one-line hook written to be spoken in the first 2 seconds.
