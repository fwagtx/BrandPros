# 09 — Monetization

**Money comes from three places, in this order:** (1) YouTube long-form ads, (2) sponsorships, (3) affiliates. Short-form creator funds are a small bonus. Everything with a threshold or percentage below is marked [VERIFY], because platforms change these terms often. Re-check them on the official help pages before planning around them.

---

## 1. YouTube Partner Program (YPP): the main goal

| Tier | Requirements (as last published [VERIFY]) | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| Early/fan-funding tier | 500 subscribers + 3 public uploads in the last 90 days + **either** 3,000 public watch hours (12 months) **or** 3M public Shorts views (90 days) | Channel memberships, Super Thanks, Shopping |
| **Full YPP (ad revenue)** | **1,000 subscribers + either 4,000 public watch hours (last 12 months) or 10M public Shorts views (last 90 days)** | Long-form ad revenue (creators have historically received 55%), Shorts ad-pool share (45%) [VERIFY] |

**The math that drives the plan:**
- 4,000 hours = 240,000 minutes.
- A 12-minute video with ~40% average view duration = about 4.8 minutes per view.
- **So you need ≈ 50,000 long-form views** (across all videos, in 12 months). Shorts views do **not** count toward watch hours.
- At 1 long-form per week, that means averaging about 2,000 views per video over the first ~25 videos, or one breakout video.
- The 10M Shorts views path is possible but less valuable: Shorts RPMs are a fraction of long-form RPMs.

**Why long-form is the business:** business and finance topics attract high-paying advertisers. Business-storytelling channels commonly report **RPMs around $5–$15 per 1,000 views** [VERIFY: varies widely by audience country, season and video length; Q4 is highest]. U.S. viewers are worth far more than international ones.

**Tactics to maximize RPM once monetized:**
- 10–15 min videos with **mid-rolls** placed at natural chapter breaks (manual placement, 2–3 per video).
- Publish flagship videos in **Q4** (Oct–Dec ad rates peak), which is exactly when this launch happens.
- Keep content advertiser-friendly: no graphic imagery, no profanity in the first 30 seconds (bleep the Costco quote), no sensational claims.

## 3. Sponsorships: the biggest lever after YPP

**When to start:** as soon as long-form videos consistently get **5,000+ views** (typically month 3–5). Don't wait for YPP, because sponsors don't require it.

**Pricing benchmark:** YouTube integrations are commonly priced on expected views × a CPM of **$20–$50** in business/finance niches [VERIFY; varies]. Example: 15,000 expected views × $25 CPM ≈ **$375** for a 60–90 s integration. Early on, flat fees of $100–$500 or CPA (pay per sign-up) deals are normal.

### Sponsor categories that fit BrandPros

| Category | Examples of the *type* of company | Fit |
|---|---|---|
| Business newsletters | Morning Brew, The Hustle, 1440 | ★★★ Perfect audience overlap; often pay per subscriber |
| Book summaries and learning | Shortform, Blinkist, Brilliant, MasterClass | ★★★ "Learn the stories behind business" |
| Investing and fintech apps | Brokerage and alternative-investment apps | ★★★ Highest payers; **requires compliance review** and clear "not financial advice" |
| Personal finance tools | Budgeting and subscription-tracking apps | ★★ Better via financeprostv, or a joint deal |
| Website and e-commerce builders | Shopify, Squarespace, Wix | ★★★ "Start your own brand" is a natural bridge |
| News and media apps | Ground News | ★★ Frequently sponsors history and business channels |
| Privacy tools | VPNs, data-removal services | ★★ Common, reliable payers |
| Productivity SaaS | Notion, Monday.com | ★ Works for founder stories |
| **Avoid** | Gambling, crypto casinos, "get rich quick" courses, MLMs | Brand-safety and legal risk; it also undermines credibility |

**Network advantage:** you run five brands. Pitch **bundles** ("BrandPros plus financeprostv, 2 videos, 1 audience of business- and money-minded 25–45s"). Bundles close faster and at a higher total value.

**Find sponsors via:** YouTube's BrandConnect (once eligible), TikTok One, Instagram's creator marketplace, sponsorship marketplaces (e.g., ThoughtLeaders, Passionfroot [VERIFY]), and **checking who sponsors similar channels** (look at the descriptions of business-history channels and pitch those advertisers directly).

>

>

> [Your name]

**Media kit** (1 page, updated monthly): audience demographics and geography, average views per long-form (last 10), average view duration, top 3 videos, follower counts per platform, network bundle option, rates, 2–3 story examples.

## 4. Affiliates and owned revenue

| Idea | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Books behind the stories** | Amazon Associates (and Bookshop.org) links in every description: *Shoe Dog*, *That Will Never Work*, *Brick by Brick*, *Sam Walton: Made in America*, *Grinding It Out*, *Sneaker Wars*, *Behind the Arches* | Low commission but zero friction; every video has a natural "read more" book |
| **Audiobooks** | Audible trial bounties [VERIFY current program] | "Listen to the full story" |
| **Book-summary apps** | Shortform / Blinkist affiliate programs | Higher payouts than Amazon |
| **"Start your own brand" tools** | Shopify affiliate program (pays per referral [VERIFY terms]), Squarespace/Wix | Use in Founder Files episodes |
| **Business newsletter (owned)** | Launch a free weekly **BrandPros newsletter** on beehiiv in month 2: one story plus 3 links | Owned audience you control, plus beehiiv's ad network, Boosts and your own sponsors. Protects you from algorithm swings. |
| **Merch (month 6+)** | "The Pro" mascot, "Pros know the lesson" and original retro designs (print-on-demand) | **Original designs only**, never other companies' logos |

**Disclosure:** "Affiliate links, we may earn a commission" at the top of the description; `#ad` / "Paid partnership" for sponsors; tick the paid-promotion box on YouTube (FTC and platform rules).

## 5. Realistic 6-month revenue roadmap

**Assumptions:** you follow the cadence (1 long-form per week from Oct 8, 2026; 1 Short per day on 4 platforms), launch Oct 5, 2026, and the audience is mostly U.S. Business RPM is assumed at $6–$10 once monetized. These are **planning scenarios, not predictions**. New channels vary enormously, and one breakout video can pull the timeline forward by months.

| Month | Period | Milestones (base case) | Revenue sources | Conservative | **Base** | Upside |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Oct 2026 | 4 long-forms live; 300–1,000 YT subs; find the best-performing series | Amazon book links | $0 | **$0–$25** | $50 |
| 2 | Nov 2026 | 8–9 long-forms; ~1,500 watch hours; newsletter launched | Affiliates | $0–$25 | **$25–$75** | $200 (a breakout video) |
| 3 | Dec 2026 | ~1,000 subs; ~3,000 hours; first CPA sponsor test; TikTok nears 10K | Affiliates, first small sponsor | $25–$75 | **$100–$350** | $1,000+ (YPP early) |
| 4 | Jan 2027 | **YPP approved** (base case); Facebook monetization applied for | YT ads (January RPM dip), 1 sponsor, affiliates | $50–$150 | **$400–$1,200** | $2,500 |
| 5 | Feb 2027 | 20+ long-forms in the back catalog; TikTok Creator Rewards live | YT ads, 1–2 sponsors, TikTok, FB | $150–$400 | **$900–$2,500** | $5,000 |
| 6 | Mar 2027 | 2 long-forms/week if quality holds; network bundle deals | All of the above plus newsletter ads | $300–$800 | **$1,700–$4,500** | $8,000+ |

**Base-case month-6 breakdown (midpoint ≈ $3,000):**
- YouTube long-form: ~150,000 views × $7 RPM ≈ $1,050
- YouTube Shorts: ~1.5M views × ~$0.06 ≈ $90
- Sponsors: 2 integrations × ~$600 ≈ $1,200
- Facebook Content Monetization (native long-form plus Reels) ≈ $300
- TikTok Creator Rewards ≈ $150
- Affiliates plus newsletter ≈ $200

**What moves you from base to upside:** a single long-form breakout (100K+ views), which usually comes from a title/thumbnail on a high-demand topic like Toys "R" Us or Sears; landing a newsletter or fintech sponsor on a recurring monthly deal; and Facebook native long-form taking off with the 45+ audience.

**What drops you to conservative:** inconsistent uploads, weak thumbnails (CTR under 3%), or a policy strike. Protect the fact-check and originality gates.
