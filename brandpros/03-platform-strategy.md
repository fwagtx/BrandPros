## YouTube (long-form): the profit center

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| Length | **10–15 min** (sweet spot 11–13). Long enough for mid-roll ads (8+ minutes) without padding. |
| Frequency | **1 per week** (Thursday 2 PM CT) for months 1–3; move to 2/week only if quality holds. |
| Format | Illustrated narrative documentary: cartoon scenes, animated charts and maps, The Pro as narrator character. Chaptered. |
| Hook style | The first 30 seconds are fully scripted: **cold open on the most dramatic moment → the thesis → a promise ("By the end you'll know why…")**. No logo intro; put the sonic logo at 0:25 at most. |
| Title style | 50–60 characters, curiosity plus a specific: "Blockbuster Could Have Bought Netflix for $50M. Here's Why It Didn't." |
| Description | The first 2 lines restate the hook with keywords; then chapters, **sources list**, a sponsor or affiliate block (disclosed), and links to related videos. |
| Tags/hashtags | 3 hashtags max above the title (#business #brandhistory #[company]). Tags matter little, so add 5–8 for misspellings. |
| Packaging | 2 thumbnails in Test & Compare. End screen to the next video in the same series plus a subscribe element. |
| Playlists | One per series. Playlists are how bingeing (and watch hours) happens. |

## YouTube Shorts

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| Length | 45–60 s (the scripts in `05-scripts/` hit about 130–150 spoken words) |
| Frequency | 1/day at 4 PM CT |
| Hook | The first 1.5 seconds carry the payoff tension ("Blockbuster had one chance to buy Netflix."). Text on screen from frame 1. |
| Caption/title | The title *is* the hook (under 70 chars) plus `#shorts`. |
| Funnel | **Use the Shorts "Related video" link** to point every Short at its long-form (or the series playlist). That's the main Shorts → long-form bridge. |
| Hashtags | 2–3: #shorts #business #[brand] |

## TikTok

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| Length | 45–60 s by default. **For Creator Rewards eligibility videos must be over 1 minute** [VERIFY current rule], so once you're eligible, make 20–30% of TikToks a 61–90 s "extended cut" with an extra beat. |
| Frequency | 1/day (6 PM CT weekdays, 12 PM weekends). Go to 2/day in month 2 if production allows; the second post can be a Brand Wars poll-style or "Name Game" quickie. |
| Hook | Text hook on screen plus spoken hook together. Faster cuts than YouTube (new visual every 1.5–2 s). |
| Caption style | One line that *adds* something (a question or a bonus fact), not a repeat of the hook. For example: "The real villain isn't who you think 👀 Which store do you miss most?" |
| Hashtags | 3–5 mixing broad and niche: #businesstok #brandhistory #nostalgia #[company] #learnontiktok |
| Engagement | Pin a comment with the source or a correction. Reply to the best comments with **video replies**; each is free new content. |
| Funnel | "Full story on YouTube: BrandPros" in the bio link and occasionally on screen. Don't overdo it: TikTok suppresses heavy off-platform CTAs. |

## Instagram (Reels plus carousels)

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| Length | Reels 45–60 s (same master as TikTok, **re-exported without the TikTok watermark**) |
| Frequency | 1 Reel/day at 10 AM CT plus **1 carousel/week** (Sunday 6 PM): a 6–8 slide illustrated "story in slides", great for saves and shares. |
| Hook | Cover frame = a mini thumbnail (cartoon plus 3 words). Hook text in the first frame. |
| Caption | 2–4 short lines: bonus fact → question → "Follow @brandpros for the stories behind the brands." Captions are searchable, so name the company. |
| Hashtags | 3–5 relevant ones. Instagram has de-emphasized hashtags, so the keywords in the caption matter more. |
| Collabs | Use **Collab posts** with @history.pros and @financeprostv (shared post, both audiences). See `10-cross-promotion.md`. |
| Stories | Daily: reshare the Reel plus a poll or quiz sticker ("Guess the year Blockbuster went bankrupt"). Feeds the algorithm a signal at no production cost. |

## Facebook (Reels, native long-form, engagement posts): the nostalgia audience

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| Reels | Same 45–60 s master, 1/day at 12 PM CT. |
| Native long-form | Upload each YouTube long-form **natively** to Facebook 2 days later (Saturday 10 AM). Facebook favors native video and it can earn in-stream ad revenue once monetized. |
| Engagement posts | 3/week (Mon/Wed/Fri 10 AM): an illustrated image plus a **memory or opinion question**. "What's something you bought at Woolworth? 👇" or "Which is the bigger mistake: A or B?" |
| Hook style | Nostalgia first, drama second. Older viewers respond to "Remember when…" more than to "You won't believe…". |
| Caption style | Conversational, full sentences, 1–3 lines, ending in a question. |
| Hashtags | 0–2. They barely matter on Facebook. |
| Avoid | Engagement bait that Facebook penalizes ("Like if…", "Share if…"). A real question is fine. |

## One master, four native versions (repurposing rules)

To avoid "reused content" flags *and* to perform natively:

| Element | YouTube Shorts | TikTok | IG Reels | FB Reels |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Video file | Master (no watermark) | Master | Master (**never** a TikTok download) | Master |
| On-screen captions | Burned in | Native TikTok captions or burned in | Burned in | Burned in |
| Text hook | Same | Same | Same | Softer, nostalgia-first variant when it's a Dead Brand |
| Caption | Title plus #shorts | Question plus bonus fact | Bonus fact, question, follow CTA | Memory or opinion question |
| Music | Licensed library track mixed in | Can add a trending sound at low volume *under* the VO | Library track | Library track |

All captions for the first 30 days are pre-written per platform in `07-content-calendar.csv`.
