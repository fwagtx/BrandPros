## What to do when something underperforms

### A single video underperforms
Diagnose by the **first broken metric in the funnel**, then fix only that:

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix (in order) |
|---|---|---|
| Low impressions | Topic has low demand, or it's too early | Wait 72 h; check search volume for the topic (YouTube search autocomplete); don't change anything yet |
| **CTR under 3%** (long-form) | Packaging | Swap the thumbnail (use the Test & Compare loser or a new concept), then retitle with a sharper number or conflict. Allow 48 h between changes. |
| **Swiped away over 40%** (Shorts) | Hook | Re-cut the first 2 s: move the most surprising line or visual to frame 1; put text on screen from the first frame. Repost as a *new* edit only if it's substantially different. |
| Retention drops at 0:30 (long-form) | Intro drags or broken promise | Tighten the cold open; make sure the title's promise appears in the first 30 s |
| Mid-video retention cliff | Slow chapter or a boring visual stretch | Note the timestamp; in future scripts, cut that type of section or add a pattern interrupt (myth check, question) |
| Good retention, few subs | No reason to come back | End with a "next story" teaser; point the end screen at a series playlist |
| Lots of views, few shares | Informative but not emotional | Add a stronger twist and a "Pros know" lesson worth quoting |
| Negative comments on accuracy | Fact error | Pin a correction within 24 h; review which gate missed it |

### A series (pillar) underperforms
Give each series a fair test: **at least 6 Shorts and 1 long-form over 4+ weeks**.
1. **Underperforming** = median views or retention **below 50% of the channel median** for 4 straight weeks.
2. **First, fix the format, not the topic:** new hook style, a different visual signature, a tighter structure, or different topics within the series (e.g., Founder Files: move from deceased founders to "the founder you've never heard of").
3. **Second, test it on a different platform:** a series that fails on TikTok may win on Facebook (Dead Brands is designed for Facebook).
4. **Third, reduce it** to occasional episodes and reallocate its slots to the top series.
5. **Double down:** the #1 series each month gets +2 slots the next month and the next long-form.

### The whole channel stalls (month 2+)
- Re-pull **best posting times** from Metricool for the BrandPros brand (replace the historypros proxy data in `03-platform-strategy.md`).
- Audit the last 10 thumbnails side by side: are they distinct at phone size?
- Check for policy issues: YouTube Studio → Monetization/Checks, Facebook Professional Dashboard → Monetization policy issues, TikTok → Account status.
- Study the 3 best-performing videos in the niche this month (other channels): topic, title pattern, length. Borrow the *pattern*, never the content.
- Increase long-form to 2/week only if Gates 1–3 still hold. Never trade quality for volume.
