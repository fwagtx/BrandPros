**Thesis:** Kodak didn't ignore digital. It couldn't afford to love it, because film was too profitable.

## Hook (0–3 s)
> **VO:** "Kodak invented the digital camera. In 1975."
> **On-screen:** `1975`
> **Visual:** A cartoon yellow film box looking shocked as a clunky toaster-sized camera appears beside it.
