    How it's made

    The pipeline from 08-production-workflow.md. Green steps run automatically; gold steps are the three human checks.

    1. Research pack

      Timeline, sourced numbers and quotes pulled from filings, books and major outlets.

      Automated

    2. Script draft

      Hook, beats, on-screen text and a cartoon scene description per beat.

      Automated

    3. Point of view

      You sharpen the thesis, the hook and the "Pros know" line.

      You · 20 min

    4. Fact sign-off

      Every [VERIFY] cleared with a source, or the line is cut.

      You · 15 min

    5. Voiceover

      One licensed brand voice, returned with word timestamps.

      Automated

    6. Scenes and motion

      Cartoon scenes in house style, camera pushes, pop cuts, stamps.

      Automated

    7. Captions and render

      Word-by-word captions and a 1080×1920 MP4, like the preview above.

      Automated

    8. Final watch

      One full watch on a phone, then approve.

      You · 5 min

    9. Schedule

      Posted to TikTok, Reels, Shorts and Facebook through Metricool with native captions.

      Automated

    Logo

    A gold price tag with the BP monogram and a rise-and-fall line: every story is a rise, a fall, or a comeback.

    Profile picture · all four platforms · reads at 40 px
    Wordmark, dark · default lockup
    Wordmark, light · for cream or white backgrounds
    • Clear space equals the height of the tag's hole on every side.
    • One-color versions: all navy, or all cream.
    • Don't recolor, stretch, rotate past 10°, or add effects.
    • Don't place it on busy imagery without a navy panel.

    The Pro

    Our narrator. A navy blazer and a price-tag head. Shows up in hooks, lessons and calls to action; never plays a real person.

    Four core poses · smile and wave (intro) · shock and hold (the twist) · think and shrug (the question) · wink and point (the lesson)

    Color

    Navy and gold carry the brand. Red and green are signals, used only for losses and gains.
