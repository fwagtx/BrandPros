Every brand has a backstory. The logo is the name, with one idea built in: the o is a pie chart with a slice pulled out, because we take companies apart and show you how the money really works. Ink, white and tangerine on top; hand-inked paper cartoons underneath. Nothing borrows another company's logo or colors.

    Founder Files cast

    Recognizable caricatures of the historical founders we cover, each drawn once, approved, and reused in every video they appear in.

    Living founders: Reed Hastings, Marc Randolph and other living executives appear as generic characters, named in the voiceover. A realistic cartoon of a living person in a scene they weren't in is the main legal risk for a channel like this.

    Logo

    The Slice: lowercase brandpros, with a pie-chart o and one tangerine slice pulled out.

    Icon · the pie on its own, for stickers, watermarks and favicons
    Profile picture · all four platforms · reads at 40 px
    Wordmark on white · default
    Wordmark on ink · white letters, lighter tangerine slice
    • Always lowercase, always Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold.
    • Clear space equals the pie's height on every side.
    • Never recolor the slice, rotate the pie or close the gap.
    • Never set another company's name in its own brand colors.

    The Pro

    Our narrator. A round face with a tangerine pie slice for a cowlick, a charcoal blazer, a tangerine tie and a pie-chart pin. Appears in hooks, lessons and calls to action.

    Color

    Ink, white and tangerine carry the brand. In the videos, red means a loss and mint means a gain.
