BrandPros Logo Options

Five wordmark-led directions. In each one the name BrandPros is the logo, with one idea built into the letters. Every direction is shown on light and dark, as a profile picture down to 32 px, on a channel page, in a thumbnail corner and on merch.

A · Rise & FallB · The SliceC · The LabelD · Story PE · The Crest
A

Rise & Fall

The A is a rising green ▲ and the O holds a falling red ▼. Every story we tell is a rise, a fall, or both, written into the name like a stock ticker.

Why it works

  • Reads instantly as BRANDPROS; the detail rewards a second look
  • Business and money without charts or dollar signs
  • The ▲▼ pair becomes a signature: series tags, transitions, merch

Watch out for

  • Keep the green and red exact; they carry the meaning
  • In one color, the triangles still read by shape
B

The Slice

Chosen

The o is a pie chart with one slice pulled out: we take companies apart and show you how the money really works.

Why it works

  • Friendly and modern lowercase
  • The pie-o is also the profile picture, simple at 32 px
  • Tangerine is rare among the brands we cover

Watch out for

  • Pie charts are common in finance, so the pulled slice keeps it ours
C

The Label

BRAND over PROS, stacked like a bold printed label with the tagline set in. Confident and newsy, like a magazine masthead.

Why it works

  • The whole name is the logo, with no symbol to explain
  • Square shape fits a profile picture perfectly
  • Strongest on thumbnails and merch

Watch out for

  • Plain on purpose; its power comes from using it everywhere, consistently
D

Story P

The P in Pros is a speech bubble: the stories people tell about brands, and the ones we tell back.

Why it works

  • Storytelling built into the letter
  • The bubble-P works alone as the icon
  • Easy to animate: the bubble pops in

Watch out for

  • Speech bubbles appear in chat apps, so keep the full name visible in most places
E

The Crest

A vintage badge with BRANDPROS arched across the top and "Stories behind the brands" underneath, like an old company seal or a varsity patch.

Why it works

  • Nostalgic, which suits the Dead Brands audience on Facebook
  • Built for hats, patches and stickers

Watch out for

  • Too detailed for tiny sizes, so the profile picture uses the center B

Pick one

  1. Judge the 32 px profile pictures first. That's how most people will meet the brand.
  2. Tell me the letter, or a mix ("A, but in C's box").
  3. I'll then apply it across the profile picture, banner, end card, video intro, thumbnails and brand kit.
  4. Before printing merch, run a USPTO trademark search on the name and the chosen logo.
