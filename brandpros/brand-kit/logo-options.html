BrandPros Logo Options

Each option has its own idea and its own typeface. Every one is shown at the sizes that matter, down to a 32-pixel profile picture, on light and on dark. None of them borrows a shape or color from the brands we cover.

How to choose

  1. Look at the 32 px versions first. That's how most people will meet the brand, in a feed or a comment.
  2. Picture it on a thumbnail corner and an end card. The mark that still feels like BrandPros there is the one.
  3. Tell me the letter (or "A's mark with B's type"). I'll roll it through the avatar, banner, end card, mascot pin, video intro and brand kit.
  4. Before printing anything, run a trademark search (USPTO) on the name and the chosen mark, and claim the handles.
