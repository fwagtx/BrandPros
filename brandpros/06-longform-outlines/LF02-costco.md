## Cold open (0:00–0:40), fully scripted
> "In 1985, Costco started selling a hot dog and a soda for a dollar fifty. It's still a dollar fifty.
> According to former CEO Craig Jelinek, when he once raised the idea of charging more, co-founder Jim Sinegal told him: 'If you raise the effing hot dog, I will kill you.'
> That sounds like a company obsessed with hot dogs. It's actually a company obsessed with something else: the little card in your wallet.
> Today, we follow the money."
