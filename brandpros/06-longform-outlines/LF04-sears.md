## Cold open (0:00–0:50), fully scripted
> "It's 1910. You live on a farm in Iowa. You need a stove, a pair of boots, a sewing machine… and a house. So you open a catalog, pick one, and mail an order form.
> A few weeks later, a train pulls in with thousands of pieces of your new home, numbered, with instructions.
> The company that shipped it had a catalog of almost everything, a national delivery network and, decades later, even an online shopping service before the web went mainstream.
> It sounds like Amazon. It was Sears. And by 2018, it was bankrupt."
