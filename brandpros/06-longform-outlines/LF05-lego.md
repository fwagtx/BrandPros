**Thesis:** LEGO wasn't saved by a new idea. It was saved by subtraction: fewer pieces, fewer side-businesses, more listening.

## Title options

## Cold open (0:00–0:45), fully scripted
> "2004. The toy company every kid on Earth knows is in trouble. Sales are falling. It's losing money fast. It has theme parks, clothing, video games, TV shows, and more than twelve thousand different kinds of pieces.
> Its owners hand the keys to a 35-year-old former consultant.
> Ten years later, that company is the biggest toy maker in the world.
> How did LEGO pull it off? By doing something almost no struggling company is brave enough to do: less."
