**Thesis:** Blockbuster's famous mistake (passing on Netflix in 2000) was defensible. The fatal mistake came in 2007, when it walked away from an online strategy that was working.

## Cold open (0:00–0:45), fully scripted
> "September 2000. Dallas, Texas. Two guys from a money-losing startup called Netflix walk into Blockbuster's headquarters with an offer: buy us for fifty million dollars, and we'll run your website.
> According to Netflix co-founder Marc Randolph, Blockbuster's CEO was trying not to laugh.
> Ten years later, Blockbuster was bankrupt, and Netflix was on its way to becoming one of the biggest entertainment companies in the world.
> So that's the story, right? Arrogant giant laughs at startup, startup wins.
> Except… it isn't. Because a few years later, Blockbuster actually started *beating* Netflix. And what happened next is the real mistake."
